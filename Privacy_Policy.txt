-----------------------
OpenChat Privacy Policy
-----------------------

OpenChat values your privacy. We do not sell your data to third parties.

-----------------------
Data Collection
-----------------------
OpenChat collects only the information necessary to provide our service, such as your account information and messages. All data is stored securely in our database and protected using industry-standard security measures.

-----------------------
Access to Messages
-----------------------
OpenChat admins do not read your private messages. The only exceptions are:

When required by law or legal authorities.

When investigating incidents that violate our Terms of Service or community guidelines.

-----------------------
User Rights
-----------------------
You may request to access, modify, or delete your account and associated data by contacting us.

-----------------------
Updates to This Policy
-----------------------
We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Users will be notified of significant changes.
